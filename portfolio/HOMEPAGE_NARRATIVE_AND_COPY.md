## 🏛️ Strategic Framework: The 3-Tier Homepage Funnel

A senior product designer's homepage must pass three sequential recruiter tests:
1. **The 5-Second Scan (Hero):** *"Who is she, what is her specific advantage, and what is she currently doing?"*
2. **The 30-Second Scan (Proof Strip & Featured Work):** *"Has she handled scale, ambiguity, and high stakes? Show me the transformation and the role, not just pretty mockups."*
3. **The 2-Minute Deep Read (Operating Principles & Career Thread):** *"How does she think when things break? What is her leadership and builder trajectory?"*

## Section 05: How I Think & Work (Operating Principles)

### Copy
```markdown
[Section Eyebrow]
OPERATING PHILOSOPHY

[Section Headline]
How I approach complex products.

1. Architecture Precedes Aesthetics
You cannot fix an ambiguous business model with beautiful drop shadows. 
I resolve data relationships, state transitions, and user mental models 
before polishing the surface layer.

2. Defensive Design by Default
Happy paths are easy. A product's true caliber is defined by how it handles 
human crisis and systemic failure: bank webhook timeouts, dead phone batteries 
at 3:00 AM, and interrupted journeys.

3. Software Is Subordinate to Reality
Digital screens are only one touchpoint in a larger human system. 
Whether coordinating warehouse runners or easing clinic reception visits, 
software must adapt to the constraints of the physical world.

4. Deliberate Friction Builds Trust
Silicon Valley preaches "zero-friction" everywhere. But in custody, 
large financial commitments, and personal safety, frictionless design is reckless. 
Deliberate pauses, verification handshakes, and transparent disclosures create 
the confidence users need to commit.
```
