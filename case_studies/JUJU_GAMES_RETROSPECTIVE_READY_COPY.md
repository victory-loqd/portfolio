## 1. The Genesis: Building a Studio Before Building a Startup

In mid-2023, independent African gaming was an untapped cultural frontier. African folklore, mythology, and modern urban realities were almost entirely absent from global gaming platforms.

We co-founded **Juju Games** to challenge the status quo: blending rich West African cultural motifs with uncompromising modern game design mechanics across PC, console, and mobile.

### My Role Beyond the Screen
As Co-Founder and Product Lead, my mandate was not limited to UI wireframing:
1. **The Creative-Engineering Bridge:** Translating abstract narrative lore and concept art into rigorous, stateful technical specifications for game engine developers in Unity and Unreal.
2. **Production Pipeline Management:** Coordinating concept artists, 3D character modelers, animators, and sound designers through iterative agile sprints.
3. **Publisher & Investor Positioning:** Authoring pitch packets, mechanical one-pagers, and demographic market analysis that positioned the studio for international indie grants, publisher showcases, and press coverage.

## 2. Project 01: *Vodou: A Space Odyssey* (Complex Systems & Telemetry)

### The Vision
A high-concept space exploration RPG fusing West African spiritual cosmology with hard science-fiction. 

### The Interface Challenge: High-Speed Cognitive Telemetry
Piloting a starship through spatial anomalies requires instantaneous situational awareness without cluttering the screen or obstructing combat visibility.

### What I Engineered:
* **The Minimalist Cockpit HUD:** An unobtrusive, peripheral heads-up display displaying shield harmonics, thruster velocity, warp heat dissipation, and dynamic orbital compassing.
* **The Spatial Inventory & Modular Crafting Economy:** A spatial grid-based inventory system requiring players to balance mass, slot geometry, and elemental crafting components.
* **Gamepad & Keyboard Parity:** Designed navigation patterns that preserved rapid 1-thumb muscle memory on controller sticks while maintaining granular mouse/keyboard precision for desktop play.

## 3. Project 02: *Boju Boju* (Stealth, Suspense & Sensory Mechanics)

### The Vision
Inspired by the traditional Nigerian game of hide-and-seek, *Boju Boju* is a stylized suspense/survival title centered on evading mythological guardians in shadow-draped cultural environments.

### The Interface Challenge: UI as Atmosphere
Traditional health bars and minimaps destroy atmospheric tension. In horror and stealth, the interface must whisper, not shout.

### What I Engineered:
* **The Proximity Sensory Radar:** Replaced intrusive 2D minimaps with an organic, audio-visual heartbeat radar that pulses subtly around the player’s character when danger nears.
* **Ambient Environmental Cues:** Light refraction and vignette shadows that communicate player visibility levels (Hidden, Exposed, Pursued) without requiring a dedicated meter.

## 4. Project 03: *Payton Ville* (Branching Quests & Narrative State Trees)

### The Vision
A narrative-driven adventure tracking interpersonal relationships, community governance, and societal dilemmas in a rapidly modernizing fictional town.

### The Interface Challenge: Visualizing Consequence
In narrative games, players need to understand how their dialogue choices alter character relationships and unlock branching story paths without spoiling future revelations.

### What I Engineered:
* **The Conversational State Tree:** A contextual radial dialogue interface that indicates tone, emotional stakes, and irreversible decision thresholds.
* **The Dynamic Quest Ledger:** A multi-layered journal separating active civic obligations, personal character relationships, and overarching community milestones.

## 7. Retrospective: How Game Development Shaped My Product Design DNA

1. **Games teach state machines better than any SaaS app:** In a video game, an interface must track real-time inputs, network latency, physics calculations, and inventory states simultaneously. That rigorous mental modeling is why architecting multi-state enterprise apps (like Skydd's 8 products or LOQD's custody loops) feels natural.
2. **True production is cross-disciplinary empathy:** Being a co-founder meant I couldn't sit in an ivory tower demanding impossible animations. I had to understand frame-rate budgets, memory limits, sound triggers, and developer velocity. That discipline makes my developer handoff in commercial product design exceptionally smooth.
3. **World-building is the highest form of brand strategy:** Whether you are building an indie game studio or a fintech platform, product design is about creating a coherent, trustworthy world that people believe in.
